Scheduled jobs not running?

macOS requires Full Disk Access for cron to actually execute jobs — otherwise it silently does nothing. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access and add cron (or your terminal app), then restart Hourglass. Use "Run" on a job to test execution immediately without waiting on the schedule.
